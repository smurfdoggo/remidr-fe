# Remidr

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

adaptive

## Product Purpose

Manage bills, record income and expenses, and track finances for a selected month. The Dashboard highlights remaining monthly budget.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Expo SDK 57, React Native, Expo Router, NativeWind, and React Native Reusables. This Dashboard uses labeled read-only sample data; backend integration and transaction-entry flows are deferred. Dashboard and Explore remain the two destinations.

Remaining budget means configured monthly budget minus recorded expenses. Income minus expenses is a separate balance. Unpaid bills are separate commitments, not recorded spending.

## Brand Commitments

Remidr uses the approved Ink Blue + Warm Ivory palette, friendly rounded typography, and a blue budget header inspired by the supplied reference.
